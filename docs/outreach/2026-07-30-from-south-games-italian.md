`Italian localization for Below, Rusted Gods — offer to contribute`

## Corpo

Hi,

Congratulations on the launch. The operator-room premise — everything reaching
the player through instruments, cameras and unreliable radio — is a genuinely
unusual structure, and it's the reason I looked at the game in the first place.

I noticed Below, Rusted Gods ships with eight languages, but not Italian.

I develop GameStringer, a desktop tool for AI-assisted game translation with
human review. I'd like to produce an Italian localization for your game and hand
it to you for free, for you to include officially if you find the quality good
enough. An Italian streamer is interested in covering the game in Italian, which
is why I'm asking now rather than in a few months.

What I would need from you is the source localization data from your Unreal
Localization Dashboard: the `Game.manifest` and `Game.archive` pair for the `en`
culture, or a `.po` export. With those I can produce a proper `Game.locres` for
`it` that plugs into the pipeline you already have.

To be explicit about what I am *not* asking: I don't want to unpack your shipped
containers, and I won't redistribute any of your assets. If you'd rather not
share those files, that's completely fine and I'll leave it there.

Happy to sign an NDA, to credit the translation however you prefer, or to hand it
over with full rights and no conditions attached. Whatever is easiest for you.

Thanks for your time,

Davide
GameStringer

## Perché è scritta così

- **Chiede una cosa precisa e piccola.** `Game.manifest` + `Game.archive` sono i
  file sorgente della localizzazione UE: uno sviluppatore li esporta in due
  minuti. "Mandami le stringhe" invece è vago e si arena.
- **Dice cosa NON farai.** Chi pubblica un gioco teme l'estrazione non
  autorizzata e le traduzioni automatiche fatte male. Metterlo per iscritto
  ribalta il sospetto di partenza.
- **Nomina lo streamer come beneficio loro**, non come tua urgenza.
- **Rende facile dire no.** Una via d'uscita esplicita aumenta le risposte, e le
  risposte negative arrivano prima — utile quando hai una scadenza.
- **Non promette date.** Non sappiamo ancora in quanto tempo lo consegneremmo.
