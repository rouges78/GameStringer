## 1. What GameStringer is — and is not

GameStringer is a **local, source-available desktop tool** that helps a person
translate a video game **they already legally own** into their own language. It
reads the translatable text from the user's own copy of a game, translates it
(via AI/LLM, OCR, or manual editing), and produces or applies a **translation**
— an in-memory text overlay, a font install, or a patch.

GameStringer **is not** a game and **does not**:

- contain, bundle, host, sell, or distribute any game or any publisher's
  copyrighted assets;
- provide, unlock, or help obtain games the user does not own;
- work on its own — it requires the user to already own and supply the original
  game.

It is developed by a **single individual, non-commercially, with no
monetization**, and released under the **GameStringer Source Available License
v1.1**.

## 2. Guiding principles

1. **Ownership first.** GameStringer is only for games the user legally owns.
2. **Personal use and language accessibility.** The core purpose is letting a
   person understand and enjoy, in their own language, software they have bought
   — including players who otherwise could not play at all.
3. **Respect for rights holders.** The project does not redistribute original
   content and responds promptly and in good faith to legitimate concerns.
4. **Non-commercial.** No sale, no ads, no paywall, no monetization of any kind.

## 3. Acceptable use — users **must**

- Own a **legal copy** of every game they use GameStringer on.
- Keep translations for **personal use**. If they choose to share their work,
  share only **patches/diffs that require the original game to apply** (e.g.
  IPS/BPS/xdelta) — never standalone copyrighted assets or wholesale copies of a
  game's original text.
- Comply with each game's **EULA / terms of service** and with the **laws of
  their own country**.

## 4. Prohibited use — users **must not**

- Use GameStringer to pirate, obtain, or distribute games or copyrighted assets.
- Redistribute substantial portions of a game's original text or assets as
  standalone content.
- Use it to gain an unfair advantage in **online or multiplayer** games.
- Use it to **defeat, disable, or circumvent** DRM or anti-cheat where this is
  prohibited by law or by the game's terms.
- Use it for any unlawful purpose.

## 5. Technical protections & anti-cheat

GameStringer is built for **single-player / offline localization**. It does
**not** disable, defeat, or bypass DRM or anti-cheat systems.

On the contrary, GameStringer includes an **anti-cheat safety gate**: before any
runtime injection it **detects known anti-cheat systems and blocks or restricts
the operation** on protected titles. This is a **guardrail** — designed to
protect users from bans and to keep the tool away from protected online games —
**not** a circumvention feature.

Users remain responsible for not using the tool in violation of
anti-circumvention rules (for example, Article 6 of the EU Copyright Directive
2001/29/EC, or 17 U.S.C. §1201 in the United States) or of a game's terms.

## 6. Community sharing (translation hub)

Where GameStringer lets users publish and download community translations, the
same rules apply to shared content:

- Shared items must be **patches/diffs that require the original game**, not
  copies of copyrighted material.
- Uploaders are **solely responsible** for what they publish and warrant they
  have the right to share it. GameStringer provides tooling — not a license to
  distribute anyone else's content.
- Integrity metadata (aggregated SHA-256) exists to verify a pack has not been
  tampered with; it is not a claim of ownership.
- Rights holders may request removal of specific shared content — see
  [Contact & takedown](#8-rights-holders--contact--takedown).

## 7. Fan-translation context (good faith)

GameStringer follows the long-standing **fan-translation** model: non-commercial,
requiring the original game, and respectful of rights holders. Projects of this
kind exist in a legal **gray area** — tolerance by publishers is common when a
project is non-commercial and does not redistribute the original work, but that
tolerance is a courtesy, **not a legal entitlement**. GameStringer is
deliberately designed to stay on the cautious side of that line: ownership
required, no content redistributed, protected/online titles gated out.
